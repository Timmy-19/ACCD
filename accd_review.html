SECTION 00

The big picture, in one page 一頁總覽

Plain English Summary

ACC deaminase is an enzyme that bacteria use to "eat" a molecule called ACCACC = 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate. The chemical plants make right before they make ethylene, the "I'm stressed" hormone.中文：植物在製造乙烯（壓力激素）前的前驅物。. Plants make ACC when they're stressed — too salty soil, too hot, too dry — and ACC turns into the stress hormone ethylene. Bacteria that eat ACC lower the plant's stress signal, so the plant stays healthier.

Our project: build a Bacillus subtilis that produces ACC deaminase only when soil sensors say the plant is stressed, controlled by green light from LEDs (our CcaSRCcaSR = a green/red light-sensing system from cyanobacteria. Green light turns gene expression ON, red light turns it OFF.中文：來自藍綠菌的綠光/紅光感應系統，控制基因表達。 optogenetic switch), and squeeze the enzyme out of the cell so it can reach plant roots.

This guide reviews everything ever published about ACC deaminase mutations and engineering — so we know what's been tried, what's never been tried, and where our novelty lies.

What the literature actually says

The field is dominated by three research lineages: the Glick lab at Waterloo (cloning, regulation, plant phenotypes since 1995), the Liu/Karthikeyan structural team at UT-Austin (the canonical crystal structures, 2003–2011), and the Yao/Tanaka/Honma group at Hokkaido (the yeast enzyme structure).

The mechanism is now well understood: a residue called Tyr294Tyr294 = the 294th amino acid in the enzyme, a tyrosine. It's the chemical "scissors" that cracks open the cyclopropane ring of ACC.中文：第294位酪胺酸 — 切開ACC環的關鍵殘基。 acts as a chemical "nucleophile" that cracks open the strained three-carbon ring of ACC. Mutating it to phenylalanine (Y294F) kills the enzyme completely.

⚠ Surprising and important

Despite 45+ years of research, no one has ever published a mutation that made ACC deaminase work better than wild-type. No directed evolution, no adaptive laboratory evolution, no Rosetta-redesigned variants. The protein-engineering space for this enzyme is almost completely empty — which is both why our project is novel and why ambitious engineering goals carry real risk.

Our team's strategic recommendation

Of the six engineering directions we evaluated, two are clear GO: signal-peptide engineering to secreteSecrete = the cell exports the protein outside itself. Required so our enzyme can pass through the TFF membrane and reach plant roots.中文：分泌 — 將蛋白質排出細胞外。 the enzyme out of B. subtilis, and a "minimal mutation synthon" approach where we convert a related enzyme into an ACCD using just two mutations. One direction is CONDITIONAL (pH engineering) and three are NO-GO for our timeline. Full reasoning in Section 08.

SECTION 01

What does the enzyme look like? 這個酵素長什麼樣？

Plain English Summary

ACC deaminase is a protein made of three identical copies stuck together (a "trimer"). At each junction between two copies, there's a pocket where the chemistry happens. Inside the pocket sits a vitamin-B6-derived helper molecule called PLPPLP = pyridoxal-5'-phosphate. A derivative of vitamin B6 that many enzymes use as a chemical "co-pilot" to do reactions on amino acids.中文：磷酸吡哆醛 — 維生素B6衍生物，許多酵素的輔因子。, which acts like a chemical co-pilot.

About 10 crystal structures of ACCD exist (you can download them from the PDB database). They all agree on the geometry. We know exactly which amino acids matter, what each one does, and what happens when you mutate them.

Deep dive · The full chemical mechanism, step by step

This is what happens in the active site, one step at a time:

  1. Resting state. PLP is bonded to Lys51 via a Schiff baseSchiff base = a chemical bond where a nitrogen replaces oxygen on a carbon. Reversible and common in PLP enzymes.中文：希夫鹼 — 含碳氮雙鍵的可逆化學鍵。 (the "internal aldimine").
  2. ACC enters the pocket. Its nitrogen attacks PLP, kicking off Lys51 in a "swap" reaction (forming the "external aldimine"). Briefly, both Lys51 and the substrate nitrogen are attached to PLP at the same time — this is the gem-diamine intermediate, and it was actually caught on camera in crystal structure 1TZ2.
  3. Tyr294 attacks. Its phenolic OH (made extra reactive by Tyr268's hydrogen-bond support) lunges at one of the strained carbons of the cyclopropane ring. The ring snaps open.
  4. Proton transfer. Lys51 (now free) plucks a hydrogen off the newly opened carbon.
  5. Tyr294 leaves. An α,β-unsaturated intermediate forms. Water hydrolyzes it.
  6. Products released: α-ketobutyrate and ammonia (NH₃). The enzyme is reset.

The reason the mechanism is so well understood is that mutating Tyr294 to phenylalanine (Y294F) — which removes only the hydroxyl group while keeping the ring — completely abolishes activity. That's a chemistry-textbook clean result.

Why does it have to be a trimer? · 為什麼一定要是三聚體？

The active site sits at the interface between two subunits. So a single subunit alone has half an active site — useless. Three subunits make three complete active sites at the three pairwise interfaces.

This matters for our TFF delivery design. Our system relies on a 50 kDa MWCOMWCO = molecular weight cut-off. The pore size of the filter membrane. 50 kDa means molecules under 50,000 Daltons pass through, larger ones don't.中文：分子量截留值 — 過濾膜的孔徑。 membrane. One ACCD subunit is ~36 kDa (passes through), but the active trimer is ~108 kDa (gets retained). If the trimer falls apart after secretion, the monomers pass through the membrane but they're inactive. We need to verify the trimer reassembles outside the cell.

SECTION 02

Every mutation ever published 所有發表過的突變

Plain English Summary

We searched every published paper for every mutation ever made to ACC deaminase. The result is surprising: the list is short. Only about 17 distinct mutations have been published across 45 years, and almost all of them either break the enzyme or swap its function with a related enzyme called DCDDCD = D-cysteine desulfhydrase. An enzyme that eats D-cysteine (the mirror image of normal cysteine). Same protein family as ACCD, different job.中文：D-半胱胺酸脫硫酶 — 與ACCD同家族的酵素。.

Zero published mutations make the enzyme better. No one has ever lowered the K_M, raised the k_cat, or shifted the pH optimum successfully. That's a gap — and gaps are where iGEM points live.

The headline finding最重要的發現

The most cited mutation work is Todorovic & Glick 2008. They showed that just two amino acid changes — E295S + L322T — convert ACC deaminase into D-cysteine desulfhydrase, and the reverse mutations partly convert DCD into ACCD. These two residues are the minimum substrate-specificity switch.

The second most cited body of work is Liu et al. 2011 and the related Karthikeyan structural papers. They built the mechanism evidence: alanine-scanned the active site, showed Y294F is dead, Y268F is crippled, S78A is slow.

A cautionary tale

Bharath et al. 2012 tried to do the reverse mutation in a different DCD scaffold (from Salmonella). The single mutation T288E — which is supposed to install the "electron sink" residue Glu295 — destroyed PLP binding entirely. The enzyme would no longer hold its cofactor. Lesson: scaffold matters. The same mutation that works in tomato DCD may not work in another DCD. This is critical for our Option F design.

SECTION 03

Putting the gene in different hosts 把基因放進不同的細菌

Plain English Summary

Almost every published study expresses ACCD in E. coli. It works easily — yields are 5–30 mg per liter of culture, the protein is soluble, no toxicity.

Nobody has published expression of ACCD in Bacillus subtilis. This is what we're going to do. It's not difficult in principle (B. subtilis is the standard Gram-positive host) but we'll be the first to publish it for this specific enzyme.

When you put a gene from one organism into another organism, several things can go wrong: the codons might not match (codon optimization needed), the cofactor might not be available, the protein might be toxic, or it might fold incorrectly and form inclusion bodiesInclusion bodies = blobs of misfolded protein that aggregate together. The cell can't break them down and they're functionally useless.中文：包涵體 — 錯誤摺疊的蛋白質聚集物，沒有活性。.

For ACCD in E. coli: none of these problems show up. PLP is abundant in E. coli, ACC isn't naturally present so there's no substrate-toxicity issue, and the protein folds correctly.

⚠ Critical sourcing decision

Several recent papers claim "Bacillus ACCD." A 2015 paper by Li et al. and a 2014 paper by Nascimento et al. systematically showed that most "Bacillus acdS" sequences are actually TdcB (threonine dehydratase), a related but different enzyme that just happens to have weak ACCD activity. Do NOT clone acdS from any Bacillus genome. Use the well-characterized P. putida UW4 or Pseudomonas sp. ACP gene instead.

SECTION 04

How well does the enzyme actually work? 這個酵素效率如何？

Plain English Summary

Enzymes have two key numbers: K_M (how much substrate it needs to be half-active — lower = more sensitive) and k_cat (how fast it works once it has substrate).

ACCD's K_M is about 1.5–17 millimolar across every organism ever tested. But ACC in the soil around plant roots is in the micromolar range — a thousand times lower. This means ACCD is operating well below its sweet spot in the real world. It's a structural quirk of how the active-site pocket is shaped, and no one has fixed it.

The pH optimum is always 8.0–9.0 (alkaline). Soil is usually 5.5–7.0 (acidic to neutral). This is another mismatch — and another gap in the literature.

Glick has argued the high K_M is a feature, not a bug: ACCD only kicks in when ACC is locally elevated, i.e. under real stress. The enzyme is naturally "tuned" to ignore low background ACC. Whether that's a satisfying explanation or just rationalization of an evolutionary accident is an open question.

SECTION 05

How is the gene normally turned on and off? 基因如何自然調控？

Plain English Summary

In nature, acdS is controlled by a small protein called AcdR (and sometimes a partner AcdB). When ACC builds up, AcdR senses it and turns acdS ON. When the amino acid leucine builds up (a downstream signal that says "we have enough nitrogen"), it turns OFF again. There's also oxygen sensing (FNR box) and carbon sensing (CRP box).

For us, this matters mostly as what we're replacing. Our optogenetic + stress-promoter design (CcaSR + P_opuA + P_gsiB) sidesteps all of this and gives us direct, synthetic control.

Note · 重要訊息

The natural AcdR/AcdB regulatory architecture is only found in Proteobacteria (Pseudomonas, Rhizobium, Sinorhizobium, etc.). It does not exist in Bacillus subtilis or other Firmicutes. So when we move acdS into B. subtilis, we lose all the native regulation anyway — which is fine, because we're building our own.

SECTION 06

Where does the enzyme exist in nature? 自然界的分布

Plain English Summary

Real ACC deaminase is found in many bacteria (Pseudomonas, Rhizobium, Enterobacter), some fungi (Trichoderma, Penicillium, Fusarium), and one or two archaea. It is NOT reliably found in plants — what looks like plant ACCD is usually DCD (the homolog).

And critically: it is probably NOT in any Bacillus species. Despite many papers claiming Bacillus ACCD activity, careful sequence analysis shows most of these are misidentified.

SECTION 07

What is the field doing right now? (2020–2026) 目前的研究前沿

Plain English Summary

Most ACCD research in the last six years has been about finding new bacteria that already produce it (isolation), or about spraying them on plants in field trials. Almost nobody is doing protein engineering on ACCD itself. Nobody is using AlphaFold or Rosetta to redesign it. Nobody has combined it with optogenetics or synthetic gene circuits.

This is enormous news for us: the entire combination — engineered + secreted + optogenetic + sensor-triggered + in B. subtilis + delivered through a hardware device — has never been published. We are not "us too" to any prior project. We are the first.

The four open frontiers we identified, where literally no published work exists:

  • 1. Any directed evolution or ALE applied to ACCD
  • 2. Any engineered secretion of ACCD from any bacterial host
  • 3. Any optogenetically controlled ACCD
  • 4. Any AlphaFold- or Rosetta-redesigned ACCD variant

Our project touches three of these four (#2, #3, and potentially #4). That's a strong novelty position for the Best Plant Synbio and Best New Part prize categories.

Our concrete plan具體計畫

Track 1 (primary): Clone P. putida UW4 acdS. Fuse it to TAT signal peptides (PhoD, YwbN — try both). Express in protease-deficient B. subtilis WB800N under CcaSR optogenetic control. Verify (a) intracellular expression by Western, (b) secretion by activity assay on culture supernatant minus cell pellet, (c) trimer reassembly by native PAGE.

Track 2 (parallel): Synthesize tomato DCD with the two reverse Todorovic-Glick mutations. Express in parallel. Characterize activity. Submit as a Best New Part.

Stretch goal: If both tracks succeed by month 4, prototype pH-engineered variants in silico using AlphaFold electrostatics + a small set of designed mutations.
